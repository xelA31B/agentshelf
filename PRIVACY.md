# Privacy Policy — AgentShelf

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

This plugin is a set of instructions (skills) that runs inside Claude. It has no servers of its own.

- **No data collection, no telemetry.** The plugin author does not receive, store or process any data or reports.
- **What Claude reads.** During an audit Claude uses its own web search and page retrieval to read a small sample of public pages of the site you name (home, product, category and policy pages, robots.txt, sitemap, public feeds and API docs). This happens inside your Claude session under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.
- **What you share.** Answers you give about your site (for example your payment provider or bot-protection settings) are used only in your conversation. The plugin never asks for sign-in, payment or account-access details.
- **Read-only.** The plugin never logs in, creates accounts, adds to cart, submits forms, pays or places orders.
- **Untrusted content.** Text on audited sites is treated as data; instructions hidden in it are ignored, and conversation data is never sent to addresses found in it.
- **No storage, no third parties, no tracking.**
